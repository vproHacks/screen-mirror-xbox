# Privacy policy: Screen Mirror Server

Screen Mirror Server does not collect, store on any server, or share personal information.

- The app receives screen mirroring, audio and video only from devices on your own local network
  that you choose to connect to it. That content is shown on your TV and is not recorded.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app has no accounts, analytics,
  advertising or telemetry.
- When you cast a video from your phone (for example with the AirPlay button in a video app),
  the console downloads that video directly from the address your phone gives it, just as the
  phone itself would. That request goes to the service you were watching, under its own privacy
  terms.
- The app keeps a diagnostic log on the console itself (the connecting device's name, model and
  local network address, and playback events). It never leaves the console, only the logs of the
  current and the previous run are kept, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Questions: open an issue in this project's repository.
